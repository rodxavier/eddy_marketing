Eddy support

Need a hand with Eddy? Email Eddy support with your device model, operating system version, app version, and crossing number.

Which devices does Eddy support?

Eddy is available for iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 16 or later. Download Eddy on the App Store. Android phones and tablets running Android 7.0 or later are coming soon.

How do I sail?

Play in portrait or landscape and swipe anywhere on the puzzle screen, or use a keyboard’s arrow keys. The Natural sea follows your swipe. The Mirror sea reverses left and right; up and down move both boats together. Reefs stop boats independently. Collect both diamonds, then reach both circular harbours together. Open Controls or Symbols from the puzzle toolbar for a reminder.

Can I change the grid layout?

In portrait, choose Side-by-side or Stacked under Grid layout in Settings or in the Controls popup inside any puzzle. Your choice is saved for all puzzle modes. Landscape always shows the grids side by side. Changing the layout or rotating your device keeps the current puzzle and your progress.

What are the striped rings?

In Endless currents, each boat that moves leaves a wake on its departing square, mirrored into the other sea. This blocks entry for one turn, then disappears. A boat already on a wake can leave. Even a blocked swipe clears old wakes. Campaign levels 1–300 and daily crossings use classic rules without wakes. Levels 301–500 and Endless use wakes.

How many puzzles are there?

Endless currents generates new 4×4, 5×5, or 6×6 puzzles on your device, checks each for a solution that uses a wake, and avoids your last 256 exact layouts. Mixed chooses one of the three sizes randomly for each new puzzle. There is no fixed level limit. The Campaign has 500 fixed levels: 100 classic levels each on 4×4, 5×5, and 6×6, then 50 wake levels on 4×4, 75 on 5×5, and 75 on 6×6. Difficulty rises within each collection, with easier introductions when the grid or rules change. Original boards and records remain available to returning players under Original crossings.

Ads

Interstitial ads may appear when continuing after a completed puzzle, with a cooldown of at least three minutes. They never interrupt a puzzle. You can also choose to watch a rewarded ad to unlock all hints for one puzzle, or spend an earned hint token. If an ad cannot load, play continues; it does not grant an ad-supported hint unlock. Remove Ads purchasers can use hints without watching an ad. Earned hint unlocks persist on this device, including after reopening or restarting. The next completion interstitial is skipped after earning a hint unlock. Undo stays free. Visit Settings for available ad privacy choices.

Remove Ads

Open Settings → Remove Ads for a one-time purchase that removes interstitials and rewarded-hint ads. There is no subscription. The App Store or Google Play displays your local price before purchase. Use Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account or Google Account and the same store to restore ownership. Purchases do not transfer between Apple and Google stores. Hints in every mode still require reaching par, the shortest solution move count. Revealing a daily hint makes that attempt practice-only. No hint tokens are needed with Remove Ads.

Daily streaks and hint tokens

Complete the daily crossing on seven consecutive UTC days to earn one hint token. Practice completions count too, once per day. After day seven, the counter resets; day eight starts a new streak at one. Missing a day resets the streak, but you keep earned tokens.

Your streak and available hint unlocks appear on the home screen. One token permanently unlocks all hints for one puzzle in any mode, without an ad. Hints still become available at par. Tokens and streaks stay on this device; Remove Ads already includes all hints.

Daily rankings

Open Daily rankings to sign in to Game Center on iOS or Google Play Games on Android. The platforms have separate rankings. Only the first attempt on this device can submit a score; fewer moves rank higher. You can resume that saved attempt, but restarting, replacing its save, or revealing a hint makes it practice-only. A first completed score cannot be improved by replaying. Eligible scores retry while the same UTC day is active; connect and open Daily rankings before midnight UTC. Reinstalling or using another device does not share attempt history. Local best scores remain available without signing in.

Stuck?

Unlocked hints show a move toward the shortest remaining solution. In every mode, make at least par attempted moves before using hints, including with Remove Ads or an existing unlock. Undo keeps those attempted moves; Restart resets the wait. Revealing a daily hint marks that day as practice-only, even after restarting. Undo and Restart are unlimited.

How are moves counted?

Daily scores count every successful move, even if you undo it. Undo itself adds no penalty. Classic and endless scores count the moves remaining in your solution. Restart begins a new local attempt.

Reporting an endless puzzle

Open Puzzle details using the information button, then choose Copy puzzle code. Include the code in your support email.

Where is my progress?

Progress is saved on your device, without an account or sync. Deleting the app removes local records, hint unlocks, tokens, and streaks. Resetting progress keeps earned hint unlocks, tokens, streaks, daily attempts, and daily hint-use status. Daily crossings change at midnight UTC.
