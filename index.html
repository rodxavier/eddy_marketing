How it works

North and south move both boats together. East and west move them in opposite directions. A reef or an edge stops only the boat that hits it.

Pick up each diamond, then reach both circular harbours at the same time. You can undo as often as you like. There’s no timer.

From Campaign level 301, and in Endless

Your last move
leaves a wake.

When a boat moves, it leaves a wake at the mirrored square in the other sea. That square blocks entry for one turn.

Use it to hold one boat back while the other moves ahead. Even a blocked move clears the old wakes.

Three ways to play

Campaign
500 levels on 4 × 4, 5 × 5, and 6 × 6 boards. The first 300 use classic rules: 100 of each size. Wakes arrive at level 301, with 50 levels on 4 × 4, 75 on 5 × 5, and 75 on 6 × 6.
Daily
One shared Classic puzzle each day. A new one arrives at midnight UTC.
Endless
New 4 × 4, 5 × 5, and 6 × 6 puzzles with wakes. Choose Mixed to vary the size. Keep playing for as long as you like.
