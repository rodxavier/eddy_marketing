How it works

Swipe either grid. The Natural sea follows your swipe; the Mirror sea reverses left and right. Up and down move both boats together. A reef or edge stops just that boat.

Pick up each diamond, then reach both circular harbours at the same time. You can undo as often as you like. There’s no timer.

From Campaign level 301, and in Endless

Your last move
leaves a wake

When a boat moves, it leaves a wake at the mirrored square in the other sea. That square blocks entry for one turn.

Use it to hold one boat back while the other moves ahead. Even a blocked move clears the old wakes.

Three ways to play

Campaign
500 crossings in 20 themed chapters, from small 4 × 4 seas to larger 6 × 6 boards. Complete a crossing to unlock the next. Wakes arrive at crossing 301.
Daily
One shared Classic puzzle each day, changing at midnight UTC. Complete seven days in a row to earn a hint token, then start a new streak. Each token unlocks hints for one puzzle in any mode.
Endless
New 4 × 4, 5 × 5, and 6 × 6 puzzles with wakes. Choose Mixed to vary the size. Keep playing for as long as you like.
